# Tingsn (霆升科技)

> Jiangsu Tingsn Technology Co., Ltd. is a Nanjing, China medical-device company focused on ultrasound for cardiac electrophysiology and structural heart disease. It builds intracardiac echocardiography (ICE) products — the TINGSN Sonic Eyes line of disposable 2D ICE diagnostic catheters and TINGSN FINDERS portable color ultrasound systems — with 4D ICE, ultrasound 3D mapping, and ultrasound ablation systems in development. Backed by Qiming Venture Partners; R&D centers in Nanjing and Massachusetts. Tingsn is a hardware/clinical company and publishes no public API or developer program.
